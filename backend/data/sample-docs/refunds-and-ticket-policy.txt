Refunds and Ticket Policy

All tickets for harbour crossings are refundable. A full refund, with no fee and
no questions asked, is available until thirty days before the date of sailing.
Between thirty days and the day of departure, the refund is the ticket price
minus a handling charge of ten percent of the value, which covers the booking
office's costs and the harbour levy that cannot be reclaimed once a passage is
reserved.

Tickets are issued in the name of the passenger and are transferable. You may
assign a ticket to another named individual of your choosing once, without a fee,
by presenting the ticket and both names at the booking office no later than
twelve hours before departure. After a ticket has been transferred once it becomes
locked to the new name and cannot be reassigned a second time.

If the ferry line cancels a crossing - for any reason at all, including a failure
of the vessel, a shortage of crew, or a problem at the destination port - every
passenger is entitled to a full refund of the ticket price automatically, with no
charge and no application needed. The refund is issued to the method of payment
that was used at the time of booking within fourteen days. In addition, the line
will cover the cost of one night's lodging for any passenger who was left stranded
by a cancellation, on presentation of a receipt.

If the harbour authority closes the sound due to weather, or the master cancels a
sailing on the grounds of sea conditions, the crossing is treated exactly like a
cancellation by the line: passengers receive the full refund and, when the
cancellation leaves them away from their home island overnight, the one night of
lodging. No claim form is required for weather cancellations; the refund is
processed automatically from the booking record the day after the intended
sailing.

Refunds are not available for baggage, pets, or vehicles carried on the deck of a
sailing vessel unless the crossing itself is cancelled. Baggage claims must be
filed separately with the cargo office and are governed by the harbour
regulations rather than this ticket policy.
